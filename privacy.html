Privacy policy

Markdown Studio for Confluence stores no personal data and sends nothing outside Atlassian.

Markdown Studio for Confluence ("the app") is provided by William Wadby, s. r. o., Tupého 34, 831 01 Bratislava, Slovakia, company ID (IČO) 53583671 ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with data. Last updated 26 October 2026.

What the app accesses

While you use it, the app reads and writes Confluence pages, spaces, attachments and labels on your site. It does this as you, with your own Confluence permissions, and only while you have the app open.

What the app stores

To resume an interrupted vault import, the app saves the import's progress: page ids and titles. It is stored in Atlassian's Forge storage for your site. It holds no names, email addresses, account ids or page content. It is deleted when the import finishes, or when you uninstall the app.

What leaves Atlassian

Nothing. The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform and has no servers of its own. Zip files you import are read in your browser, and only the resulting pages and attachments are written to your Confluence site.

What we can see

We have no access to your Confluence content or your users' data. Atlassian gives us licensing and installation details for the site, such as the site address and subscription status, which we use only to provide and bill for the app. When you contact support, we keep what you send us for as long as we need it to answer you.

Your rights

Because the app stores no personal data, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Under the GDPR you can still ask us about your data at any time, and you can complain to the Slovak data protection authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky).
