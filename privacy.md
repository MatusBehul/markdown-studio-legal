# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

This policy covers **Markdown Studio**, an app for Atlassian Confluence Cloud provided by William Wadby ("we", "us").

## The short version

Markdown Studio runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see your Confluence content.

## What the app does with your data

- **Pages you edit or create.** When you use "Edit as Markdown", "New page from Markdown" or the vault import, the app reads and writes Confluence pages, attachments and labels on your behalf, using the permissions you approved when installing it. That content stays in your Confluence site.
- **Vault files.** A zipped Obsidian vault is opened in your browser. Only the pages and files it creates are sent, and they go to your Confluence site.
- **Import progress.** So that an interrupted import can resume, the app saves its progress (page titles, page ids and error messages) in Forge app storage, which Atlassian hosts for your site. It is keyed by the vault and destination, not by who you are, and it is deleted when the import finishes.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, store or sell personal data.
- The app makes no calls to servers outside Atlassian.
- We do not use analytics or tracking in the app.

## Atlassian

Atlassian hosts the app and your data. Their handling of data is described in the [Atlassian Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Removing data

Uninstalling the app removes its stored import progress. Pages and attachments it created are normal Confluence content and stay until you delete them.
